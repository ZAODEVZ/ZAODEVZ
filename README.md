# The ZAO

**An incubator for music and culture projects that runs on social capital, not money first.**

People earn standing in the ZAO by showing up and doing the work. That standing decides who leads, which projects get backed, and who wears which role. Projects start as a spark, grow on real support, and launch a token only if it ever makes sense.

## How it works

### 1. The ZAO Fractal: earn Respect
Every Monday at 6 PM ET, since August 2024, members meet in small groups, share what they did for the ZAO that week, and rank each other. The rankings mint **Respect**, onchain on Optimism. Respect is our social capital: it cannot be bought, only earned, and it is the weight behind ZAO decisions.
[fractal.thezao.com](https://fractal.thezao.com) · [ZAOfractal](https://github.com/ZAODEVZ/ZAOfractal)

### 2. Sparkz: start with a spark, not a token
Every ZAO project opens a **Hearth** on Sparkz: a home for its identity, its backers and a record of everything it ships. People back the work with boosts and receipts, no coin and no wallet needed. A token comes last, if ever.
[trysparkz.com](https://trysparkz.com) · [sparkz](https://github.com/bettercallzaal/sparkz)

### 3. Hats: your role, onchain
Roles in the ZAO are hats on the ZAO Hats tree (Optimism). A free hat is your first commitment to a project; more hats come as you build.

**Fractal = who decides. Sparkz = what gets built and backed. Hats = who you are.**
